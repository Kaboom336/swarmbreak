   - Auto-aim plus auto-fire kills them at the gates, 60–70 studs out, before they reach camera range. The only combat signs are "WAVE CLEAR +10 GOLD" and "WAVE 2" text.

2. **Card panels cover the game constantly.**
   - In the 10-second sheet, **about 12 of 30 frames show a 3-card panel** (DMG/HP/SPD, MAG/DMG/RPM, ELEM/HP/MAG…).
   - A new level every few seconds, plus the 8 s auto-pick, means the centre of the screen is blocked most of the time.
3. **The player stands still on the H pad nearly the whole session.** The game never asks them to move (no threat, no pickups to chase, no objective), so the camera shows the same static view for minutes.
4. **The map reads as props in rows.**
   - Cargo stacks and modules sit along the walls in evenly spaced lines.
   - The centre is an empty cross of asphalt lanes.
   - Some primitive bits remain (the dark roofed slab, plain blocks).
   - Zion's "scattered, not placed with thought" fits: there are no landmarks, no paths through cover, and no reason to go anywhere.
5. **Lobby:** a white floor with blue sticks, discs and cylinders scattered everywhere (the "blocky particles" Zion reported earlier). Flat text boards. No clear way to the START ring.

## Root causes (inferred)
- Auto-aim range and DPS are far above what the arena needs. Enemies are fodder that never close in.
- Level-up XP thresholds are too low for a 3-wave stage.
- The camera sits at the pad centre and never shows enemies arriving.
- Map art is placed for looks from above (wide shot), not for play lines at player height.
- The whole design was iterated on primitives, without a reference wave-game blueprint for spawn rhythm, enemy approach and cover use.
