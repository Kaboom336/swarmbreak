## What these winners share (from the frames)
- **Thumbnails:**
  - A bacon-hair avatar mid-action;
  - one big object (an egg, a stone, a rainbow item);
  - huge stroked numbers ("+1", "LEVEL 999", "0.001%", "+9.2M");
  - flat bright skies and grass or water.

  This is one house style across all of them.
- **In game:**
  - Simple bright worlds (grass and water, desert, snow).
  - The HUD is full of numbers: a top progress bar, left stat stacks (power, cash, gems), "Friend Boost +0%".
  - Big chunky buttons (Drop / Teleport to Base / Rebirth / Pet Size).
  - Constant "+N" popups.
- **Loop:**
  - One verb (break, skip, drill, peel, carry), then a number goes up, then a zone or tier unlocks.
  - Pets or animals as passive multipliers. Rebirth.
  - Light social pressure (stealing, a guard chase, a shared server goal).
- **Monetisation:**
  - **Multiplier and zone game passes** (2x gym, admin zone, x2 money).
  - **Hatch-count stacks**, plus VIP tiers from 160 to 4500 R$.
  - Auto-farm passes (Auto Rebirth, Auto Wins).
- **Scope:**
  - All 5 were created **within the last 5 weeks** and already have 10–48K concurrent players.
  - One map, one loop, one verb, built on proven templates (several say "inspired by …").
