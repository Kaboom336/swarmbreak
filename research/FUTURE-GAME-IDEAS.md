# Future Roblox game ideas (running list, Zion 2026-10-02)
Rule: while studying games for Swarm Break, add any idea for a future game here, with its source and why it could work. Ideas are unvalidated until checked against live player counts and a quick prototype. Prefer ideas that reuse Swarm Break systems (Rojo setup, tests, Economy, Quests, Crates, HUD kit, movement, Blender pipeline).

| # | Idea | Inspired by | Why it could work | Reuses from Swarm Break |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Swarm Defense: tower-defense spin-off with the same swarm, bosses and elements | Tower Defense Simulator | Same enemies and art, a proven genre, and a different player type (planners) | Enemies, elements, waves, Crates, Quests |
| 2 | Simple-art collect-and-grow loop with restock timers (e.g. raising creatures on a farm) | Grow a Garden | The biggest recent hit used default studs; the loop and timers did the work | Economy, Daily rewards, HUD tabs |
| 3 | Journey survival: travel a long route (boat, bus) fighting off threats, with in-world gauges | Dead Rails | Clear goal (reach the end), co-op, simple low-poly art | Waves, co-op bonus, Kits |
| 4 | Fast arena PvP built on our movement kit | Rivals | Movement and hit feel carry it; maps can be simple blocks | Movement (T7), weapons, hit feedback, skins |
| 5 | Co-op light horror: short floors with one strong light per room | Doors | Mood comes from lighting, not detail, so art cost is low | Lighting presets, tutorial, Quests |
| 6 | Keep the power core alive: co-op space survival | 99 Nights in the Forest | One shared visible goal; the map grows as the core charges | Waves, co-op, Kits |
| 7 | Steal-and-defend base game with sci-fi critters | Steal a Brainrot | Social tension and raids drive sessions | Economy, Base lobby |
| 8 | Anime tower defense with seasonal events | Anime Vanguards | Huge genre with polished summons; crowded, so it needs a hook | Crates, pity, events |
| 9 | Calm space fishing with server-wide rift events | Fisch | Simple loop plus timing minigame and events | Economy, Quests |
