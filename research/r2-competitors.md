Three readings that are new:
1. **Spike then decay is the shape of this genre.** Final Swarm hit 20,107 a week ago (the CLASSES update, code Cl4ssy added Sept 28) and is at 2,399 today; its 7-day peak is 8,845 and 24h peak 3,198, so the update spike lost ~85% in a week (computed from rolimons). 100 Waves Later peaked at 11,301 and is at 184 with a 98.3% rating: a good rating does not stop decay; only new updates do.
2. **The breakout is six weeks old.** Build and Kill Zombies was created Aug 19 2026 and peaked at 139,882 within six weeks, with 896K favorites. Proof that a new wave game can still break out in 2026 (not that we will).
3. **Sessions are 10-19 minutes.** Average playtime: Final Swarm 10.7, 100 Waves Later 14.5, BaKZ 19.1. One full run of Swarm Break (to the wave 25 win) should fit in about 12-15 minutes (est. target) so a first session has a win in it.

## 2. Game by game (what they do that we don't, one thing we do better)

### Build and Kill Zombies (Zombie Car Crusher): https://www.roblox.com/games/105011592530400
- Loop: build phase at two **roll machines** (CAR PARTS rolls Blocks, Engines, Wheels, Fuels; SUPPORTS rolls weapons and add-ons), then drive into the horde and push your best distance in **Studs** ([fan wiki](https://build-and-kill-zombies.github.io/)). Parts are gacha only; a persistent **Luck** skill improves what the machines give and survives rebuilds (same source).
- Bosses: scheduled, with a **"NEXT BOSS IN" countdown on the HUD**; Update 3 is the Zeus Event with a Zeus boss fight and lightning tokens (same source; ZEUS code added Sep 28, [pockettactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/build-and-kill-zombies-codes)). Three numbered updates in about six weeks.
- Codes as marketing: MOREFRIENDS (passenger seats, i.e. bring a friend), IAMPRO (5K cash, **only after 1K studs**, a code that rewards play), expired NEWBOSSNEXTWEEK (a **teaser code** for the next update) (pockettactics).
- Does that we don't: one bragging number (distance), a persistent luck stat, a boss countdown always on screen, codes that point at the next update.
- We do better: our weapons are aimed and fought with; theirs is a drive-and-watch. Show our "one number" (best wave + boss kills) on the lobby board and in the run summary.

### Final Swarm (Evolution Studio Games): https://www.roblox.com/games/99521272836282
- Store page sells four things: hundreds of enemies, keys to unlock weapons, "best weapon loadout", worlds; plus a limited cosmetic set and a group reward ([roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/99521272836282/Final-Swarm)).
- Update ladder: at least **Update 8 [Raids]**, a [Skills] skill-tree update in Aug 2026 ("Keys unlock weapons and skill tree nodes while Chests power up your run"), Jungle world, then [CLASSES] (Sept 28) ([finalswarm.wiki](https://finalswarm.wiki/), [theclick](https://www.theclick.gg/final-swarm-codes/)). Roughly one named update per month since January (est. from 8+ updates in 8.5 months).
- Codes hand out chests, not currency: Cl4ssy and ADMIN = 1 Mythic chest, ex0tic = 10 Golden, INF3RNO = 3 Inferno, QOL2 = 10 Raid chests + 150 Keys (theclick).
- Does that we don't: raids, a key-bought skill tree, chest codes, a limited cosmetic set.
- We do better: average playtime is only 10.7 min. Our run summary + "next goal" screen (design gauntlet round 1) is aimed exactly at the second run they lose.

### Survive The Swarm (solo dev, FAKT): https://www.roblox.com/games/100227226022278
- Store page now lists the six classes plus "synergy mechanic to unlock weapon evolutions", co-op, **weekly updates (bugs expected)**, Discord codes, console ([roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/100227226022278/Survive-The-Swarm)).
- New since RESEARCH.md: World 2 and a SINGULARITY update; codes now give Coins, Essence and a **Gilded Egg** (pets); HYPETMRW and WORLD2HYPE were **"hype" codes released before the update** ([destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/survive-the-swarm-codes/), [gamertweak](https://gamertweak.com/survive-the-swarm-codes/)).
- Does that we don't: classes, pets from eggs, a second world, console, pre-update hype codes.
- We do better: it is a one-person game that admits bugs on its store page. Our tested pure modules (67 tests) let us ship weekly *without* "bugs expected".

### Zombie Rush Survival: (rblxdb rank 4, 5,327 live, 121.3M visits)
- Wave shooter, solo or team, money spent **mid-run** on firepower and defenses; Credits after the run buy **classes at rising prices: Rookie free, Medic 50K, Recon 100K, Trapper 200K, Pyro 300K, Demolitionist 400K**, each with three deployables (turrets, barricades, traps) ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/zombie-rush-survival-classes-guide-costs-and-equipment/)).
- Does that we don't: classes as the long grind ladder, placeable deployables.
- We do better: classes behind a 400K grind are a wall. If we add kits, price the first two cheap enough to unlock in the first two sessions.

### Survive the Apocalypse: (rblxdb rank 3, 7,040 live, 266M visits)
- Day/night: scavenge fuel by day, build and repair by night, survive waves, **escape on Day 100**; 22 classes with 3 upgrade levels each, unlocked with Emeralds; Blood Moon brings mutated bosses; Chapter 2 (Aug 2026) added vehicles, **weekly leaderboards**, Hard Mode and Perks ([wiki](https://survivetheapocalypse.wiki/)).
- Does that we don't: a named escape goal, Hard Mode, weekly leaderboards that reset.
- We do better: their runs are long (100 days); ours hits a boss every 5 waves and a win at 25. Keep that short arc.

### Hunty Zombie (rblxdb rank 6, 2,742 live, 711.6M visits)
- Melee-weapon zombie co-op; weapons from a **spin** (Sledgehammer 2.4% Normal Spin, 36% Lucky Spin); a skill tree of Attack/Defense/Crit/Speed where buffing one debuffs another; **Payload mode** (escort a bus, fix tires, clear roadblocks; 1-3 tokens); guilds in the current title ([progameguides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/hunty-zombie-payload-update-complete-guide/), [rblxdb](https://rblxdb.com/best-roblox-games/zombie)).
- Does that we don't: a second objective mode, guilds.
- We do better: it publishes odds per spin type, which we also do; we can add a mode cheaply because our arena has gates and a Nest: "hold the gate" is our Payload.

### Horde Defenders, 100 Waves Later, Hellreaver Arena (short; detail in the previous R2 pass)
- Horde Defenders (https://www.roblox.com/games/93237333081860): 18 self-aimed towers, 3 heroes, 59 levels over 5 worlds, Endless, 153-node tech tree ([wiki](https://www.horde-defenders.wiki/)). We could do better with a handful of fixed-rule missions on our one arena instead of 59 handmade levels.
- 100 Waves Later (https://www.roblox.com/games/92371631484540): weapon and skill rolls each wave, wave 100 in the name, 630K likes; now 184 live. Lesson: a finish line in the title works for launch, not for retention.
- Hellreaver Arena (https://www.roblox.com/games/5523314295): movement-heavy arena FPS, 97K likes, no update for 3 months, 1 player. Not our loop; the same lesson.

## 3. What the leaders share that we still lack (new items only)

1. **Update spikes are the business.** Final Swarm went 2.4K to 20K to 2.4K around one update. Plan each weekly drop as an event: title tag, a code, a thumbnail, and a *teaser code the day before* (HYPETMRW, WORLD2HYPE, NEWBOSSNEXTWEEK).
2. **Codes that do a job**, not only "free stuff": MOREFRIENDS (bring a friend), IAMPRO (unlocks after a milestone). Our Codes module should support a requirement field (e.g. reached wave 10).
3. **A boss countdown on the HUD** ("NEXT BOSS IN"). We have a boss every 5 waves; show "Boss in 2 waves" permanently.
4. **A persistent luck or skill-tree stat** that improves future rolls or runs (BaKZ Luck, Final Swarm and Hunty skill trees). Our Gems/shards could buy a small tree; keep it to 3 branches x 3 nodes.
5. **Pets or eggs from codes** (STS Gilded Egg) and **chests from codes** (Final Swarm). Our code rewards should be a crate or shards, not only Gems.
6. **A second mode** (Payload, Raids, Hard Mode). Ours: "Hold the gate" or Hard Mode on the same arena.
7. **Weekly leaderboards that reset** (Survive the Apocalypse Chapter 2) so new players can place.

## 4. Five things to do, ranked (complements the previous R2 list: codes, escape portal, title tag, missions, kits)
1. Codes with a requirement field and a "hype" code for the day before each drop.

4. Weekly best-wave leaderboard in the lobby that resets Monday.
5. A 3x3 Gem skill tree (damage / survival / luck on crates) as the first post-launch drop.
