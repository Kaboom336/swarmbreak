## Takeaways
1. Professional-looking Roblox games use **specialist-made VFX and systems** (VFX artists, marketplace kits), not code-generated parts.
2. Our VFX should be built from **mesh crescents/slashes, debris, world-darken and camera work**. Get there by buying or harvesting proper VFX packs, or hiring a VFX artist.
