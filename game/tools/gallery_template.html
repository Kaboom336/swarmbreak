Swarm Break: the models so far

What changed and what is next

Why the models changed: the first passes looked like balloon animals next to real games. We rendered 39 open-licence game models (Kenney, KayKit, the Khronos fox) in our own lighting as the bar, measured them (100 to 1500 triangles, big flat colour shapes, huge heads) and rebuilt the recipe: faceted low-poly, armour cut from the body, big glowing eyes, dark body with one bright accent, baked shading, a thumbnail-style render. Judge agents score every model against that sheet; nothing ships under 8 of 10.

Names are plain: Nature, Tech, Lightning, Dark for the four evolution paths; Gems and Shards for the forever currencies; the lobby is the Base; the station is Outpost 9; the hive under it is the Nest.

Next: walk cycles (skinned animation), particle textures, the Base lobby place, music. Then the laptop step: import the FBX files, playtest, publish.
