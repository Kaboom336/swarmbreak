The story why nothing bleeds

Aster-9

A research station drilled into a fracture under the ice. The crew called it the Rift. It leaks rift-light: raw energy that takes a body when it touches something living or built. Left alone it becomes the Swarm. Caught and shaped, it becomes a weapon.

Breakers

The survivors sealed the lower decks and opened the Break Room. From there, Breakers drop into the arena decks below. Every enemy is rift-light wearing a body: when it dies it breaks back into motes of light. Bosses break into a storm of it.
