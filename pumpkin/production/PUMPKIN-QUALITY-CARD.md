## Capture route and adjudication

Use one identified build for editor framing; solo spawn/harvest/cast/contact/burst/reward/upgrade/second outing; matched small/big views; two-client intentional chains; phone view; reduced effects; sound on/off; reset/rejoin. Keep a continuous solo clip so waiting and confusion are visible, not edited away. Save outputs and logs with the build hash.

Each review returns: observed difference, player consequence, largest useful repair and evidence needed to judge the repair. Do not invent numerical scores while either side is unobserved. Do not try to beat every genre at every dimension: choose relevant standards for this game's fantasy and judge a coherent outing. Every exposed detail should have an owner and a real acceptance check; the comparison backlog expands with new content.
