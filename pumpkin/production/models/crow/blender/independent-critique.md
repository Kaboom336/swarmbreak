# Independent visual critique

Reviewed actual front and three-quarter renders from iterations 1, 2, and 3; iteration 2 and 3 side renders; the original Pup A and Pet Simulator 99 references; and the iteration 2 reference comparison sheet.

**Keep iteration 3.** This is a visual judgment, not owner approval.

- Silhouette: readable large rounded brick head, short body, tiny feet, and raised wings. Iteration 2 introduced feather steps and an asymmetrical wave; these avoid the bent robot-arm impression of iteration 1.
- Face: iteration 3's thinner eye discs remove much of the raised-goggle appearance. The huge eyes and paired highlights remain readable when reduced. The tapered orange beak and tiny seam provide a clearer bird face.
- Proportions: convincingly chibi. The smaller bib in iteration 3 preserves the black body and simplifies the colour hierarchy.
- Style match: rounded block forms, restrained studs, stepped feathers, and pumpkin stem relate to Pup A. Clean large colour blocks and large eyes relate to the Pet Simulator 99 readability reference. The unified head avoids the rejected lumpy primitive-stack construction.

Remaining limitations: the pumpkin hat remains fairly flat; the face is simpler and less expressive than Pup A's layered muzzle and brows; the front-render hat highlight still washes part of the orange toward white. None warrants adding decorative blobs. Owner preference is UNKNOWN until Zion reviews it.

No Roblox Studio import or in-game appearance test was performed by this critic. Export geometry, scale, normals, and triangle counts are outside this visual review.

Lessons: when making a small pet, change the outer silhouette before adding surface detail because feather steps survive thumbnail scale. When eyes resemble goggles, reduce depth before shrinking them because size drives cute readability. When matching an approved brick pet, reuse its form language rather than copying unrelated facial anatomy.
