Lessons:
- When a hero gem is inherited, audit its whole projection too, because a safe root can still overhang a path.
- When lamps sit near crossings, check all routes before housing them, because one clear shoulder can block another lane.
- When glow flattens a gem, retain darker facets and luminous seams, because contrast gives it volume.
