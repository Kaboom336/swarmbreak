# Cave Round 2: three independent blind reviews

Reviewers were fresh agents with no conversation history. Each read only concept-v2.1, the comparison, and the current concept/side/top views. These are offline native-manifest renders, not Studio captures. No passing score was suggested to reviewers.

| Review | Overall | Mass | Underside | Dressing | Lighting |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4.5 | 4 | 5.5 | 3 | 4 |
| 2 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| 3, final | 5.5 | 6 | 6 | 4 | 5 |

Review 1: broad flat apron dominated, summit terrain was too shallow, underside too regular, mining props too small and sparse, crystals lacked illumination. Corrections: compact lobed terrain, substantial stepped summit/west shoulders, destination scaffold/crate/cart groups, stronger native lights and their offline proxy illumination.

Review 2: continuous cubic perimeter, repeated crystal ornaments, dark roof/bridge/perimeter gaps, tangled junctions. Corrections: stronger rock peaks, faster underside taper and narrower hanging lobes, remove high rock supports concealing the canyon, varied destination crystal groups, native crystal facet edges, spiral portal and lit tunnel interiors.

Final review: readable route loop, strong summit landmark, warm/cool separation, recognizable portal and floating silhouette. Remaining defects: coarse sparse surface detail, crystals appearing as separate bundles, and dark roof slabs/holes dominating the central activity area. Prominent cave arches, banners, crates and camp art are authored but not sufficiently visible at this camera distance. The concept's creature and characters remain absent. Final score 5.5/10; target >=7 was NOT met. No fourth review.

After final review, only top-camera framing was widened to include the northern rock mass and floating satellites; native geometry was unchanged.
