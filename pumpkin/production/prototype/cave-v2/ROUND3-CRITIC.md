# Round 3 blind reviews

Two independent agents received only the reference/build image paths and requested visual criteria. They did not receive the build source, prior scores, implementation history, or target score. Height and mass were accepted constraints. No third review.

## First review — blind_critic_a — 6/10

Checked concept-v2.1.png against build-rr3-a-concept.png. Crystal and lamp coverage increased, paths clearly read, but broad shelves felt empty. Neon faces were flat, mine scenes sparse, rock blocks smooth, and distant setting underdeveloped. Requested layered islands and bridges, darker crystal facets, deliberate prop groups and finer exposed rock.

## Final review — blind_critic_b — 7/10

Checked concept-v2.1.png against build-rr3-final-concept.png. Strong purple/cyan coverage, warm route network, readable voxel faces and visible rails, timber supports, fences and portal. Peripheral islands and lantern bridges establish the floating mine setting.

Residual gaps: emissive surfaces dominate over faceted depth and light spill; repeated clusters feel distributed rather than embedded in chosen seams. Infrastructure reads better than purposeful mining scenes. Broad terraces, repeated square lanterns, checkerboard rock and black voids weaken the finish. Simple background islands and flat blue sky have less atmosphere than the reference.

Target 7/10 met; this is a visual comparison of an offline native-geometry render, not a Studio lighting or performance certification.
