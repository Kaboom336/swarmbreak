# Independent image review

Checked only `READABLE-SHEET.jpg` and `concept-v2.1.png`. Scores are visual inference, not a measured player test. Studio lighting, collision and actual traversal are UNKNOWN.

- **Where to go in 3 seconds: 7.0/10.** The portal view exposes a broad left walking ramp, a central brown rail route and a right stone cave landmark. The main mouth view has a continuous pale path framed by a green gate; the cart mouth has unmistakable parallel rails; the cave mouth has a signed arch and visible dark entrance. These give a plausible immediate choice. Recognizing the next path is stronger than understanding each destination.
- **Look quality and resemblance to concept: 5.5/10.** The cool blue night, cyan/purple cliff crystals, floating slate island and yellow lantern rhythm reproduce the concept's large color and silhouette cues. The far view is the strongest resemblance evidence. At player height, rock, paths and arch look like rough block construction, with sparse mine dressing and weak warm light pools compared with the richly crafted concept.

## Three specific shortcomings

1. **Walking and rail boundaries blur at the junction.** The portal view puts rails on the same pale deck as adjacent walking routes. In the cave mouth view, rail ties and diagonal loose-looking pieces cross the foreground walking edge. They read as possible obstructions or route clutter; collision cannot be determined from images.
2. **Destinations are less legible than route mouths.** The green main sign is low contrast in the portal view, while the main walking view leads toward a bend and crystal hill without a distinct camp payoff. The cave sign is readable, but the oversized arch and nearby dark rock blocks make the actual entrance smaller and less inviting than the concept's glowing doorways.
3. **The material and landmark finish falls well short of the concept.** Broad flat tan tiled paths, repetitive squared slate blocks, black gaps in the arch, solid neon crystal faces and mostly isolated yellow lantern squares lack the concept's textured masonry, crystal facet detail, timber braces, fences, crates, banners and amber illumination across nearby surfaces. This is a recognizable structural translation, with a substantial art pass still needed.

## Strongest evidence

- Readability: the main mouth's broad uninterrupted ramp and cart mouth's paired rails establish distinct movement cues without relying on the annotated top plan.
- Resemblance: the far three-quarter view retains the floating stepped island, purple/cyan crystals on top and below, cool night palette and lantern-lined ascending routes.
- Remaining gap: the concept's dense timber-and-stone mine landmarks and warm surface lighting are immediately visible side by side with the sheet's comparatively bare player views.
