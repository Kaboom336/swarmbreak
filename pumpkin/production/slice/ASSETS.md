## Intake gate and lessons

Record original licence file, source/creator/ID, zero cost, exact selected piece, descendants scan, mesh/texture totals, measured size, approved concept/shot and manifest hash. Missing rights or scripts fail intake; missing final art fails loudly rather than falling back to Parts. Generated hero terms and Store redistribution remain UNKNOWN until checked.

- When a Store page reports a script, exclude it before insertion because free does not mean script-free.
- When a shell must open, author curved separated geometry because a stock pumpkin cannot prove hatch readiness.
- When sourcing a family pack, inspect exact pieces before promising a match because pack tags are not an item manifest.
