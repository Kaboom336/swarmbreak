# PROGRESSION PLAN (arena r21, PA + PB merged)

Builds on R20-PLAN and BOSS-PLAN (King Night stays the only boss).

## Ghost raids
- No raids in the first 15 min of play.
- 30 s warning: bell + one queued banner "Ghosts coming! Lock base or go home".
- Skip the base if the owner is Locked, within 60 studs, or **idle 45 s+** ("Zzz" shield). Raids only hit active kids who are out exploring.
- Max ONE item: lowest-rarity placed pet. Never Epic+, never a pet under 5 min old. One raid per base per 20 min.
- The pet rides a **Stash Ghost** over your base until you return; tap it 5x to get it back. Income pauses until then; nothing is lost for good.

## Zone ladder (2 pets per zone; * = new recolor)
| Zone | Arrive (min) | Pets | Unlock |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Meadow | 0 | PumpkinPup, Crow | R20 tutorial |
| 2 Graveyard | 5 | Ghosty, BonePup | **Fuse Machine**: 3 same -> 1, kg x1.5, 10% Shiny |
| 3 Woods | 15 | BlackCat, Moth* | Pen slots 7-8 |
| 4 Swamp | 30 | FrostOwl, Toad* | **Witch Merchant**: 3 rotating pumpkins for candy, restock 5 min |
| 5 Castle | 50 | GoldenPup, CandyBat | Pen slots 9-10 |
| 6 Candy Land | 80 | LavaSlime, GummyBear* | **Gold mutation**: 1 in 12 hatches Gold (2x income) |
| 7 Throne | 120 | KingCat, PumpkinDragon* | **Rebirth** |

- Index stamp row per zone: own both zone pets = permanent +10% candy (max +70%).
- Gate billboard: "2 new pets + <unlock>".
- Rebirth: needs 1 Legendary + 5M candy. Resets candy, scythe, gates; keeps pets, pen, stamps. +25% candy each, cap 10.
- Economy: tune gate costs (start 1k/25k/400k/2.5M/17.5M/140M) with a quick sim so active players with the matching scythe hit the arrival minutes.

## Zone quests
Same 3 checkboxes per zone: smash 50 here, steal 1 here, own 1 zone pet. Reward: -25% on the next gate. Zone 7 quest unlocks Rebirth.

## Dropped
Offline-base raids (hard to build, no tension for online kids); per-zone mini-bosses (BOSS-PLAN forbids); Pet Team (duplicates pen income); King Night locked to Zone 7 (it is open to all).

## Delayed
World 2, pet followers, feeding, Robux items, extra rebirth perks.
