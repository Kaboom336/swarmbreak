### Candy jumps and drops explained
- **69.9k to 280.8k (log 1222-1252):** this was not King loot. It was 3 x "Swap weakest pet" in a full pen. Each swap paid about 91% of the stolen pumpkin's value (103,950 to +94,500; 83,160 to +75,600) **and** placed the pumpkin to hatch (income 591 to 864/s). The player got both the money and the pet.
- **438k to 334k (log 1372-1402):** the player bought the Candy Scythe for 400k (to 50.1k), then 3 swaps returned about +253k.
- Every other rise was passive income (591-869/s, about 6-9k per 10 s row). Smashing added under 1% (pops of +1 to +20).
