## The candy drop (336k to 25k, log 1132-1142): a gate purchase, not a bug

- **The numbers.** Log 1132: candy 336,402 at position (1457,3,355), in the Graveyard by the far gate. Log 1142: candy 25,128 at (1475,3,**520**), past the gate and inside the Haunted Woods.
- **The price.** The Haunted Woods gate costs **318,000** (`giant/src/ReplicatedStorage/PatchConfig.luau:1206`, `gateCosts = {1000, 318000, 3064000, ...}`). 336,402 + about 3.1k of income + the 3.2k quest reward − 318,000 ≈ 25k. The numbers match exactly.
- **What the frames show.** The last frame (4:50) shows the objective "Unlock the Haunted Woods! Costs 318k" and the smash quest paying "+3.2k". The purchase happened about 1-8 s after the recording ends, so it is in `play_002` (still being written).
- **Side effect.** The gate also adds pen slots 7-8 (billboard "2 new pets + Pen slots 7-8"; the player now has `PenSlots=8`). Stashed pumpkins auto-filled them, so **income jumped from 314.2 to 591.4/s** at log 1152 with no delivery.
- **Verdict.** Working as designed. The problems are the design: 92% of the bank goes in one touch or prompt, with no confirm and no visible payoff (see S2-06), and the next gate is 3.06M (see S2-29).
