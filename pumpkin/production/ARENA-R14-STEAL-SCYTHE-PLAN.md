# Arena round 14 verdict: Pumpkin Patch Panic = Steal an Egg + scythe farming (2026-10-08)

Proposals: arena/r14/PSteal.md (steal-first), PScythe.md (scythe-first). Both converge; merged plan below.

## Loop
- 30 s: swing your scythe at wild pumpkins in a zone (HP bars, Pet Sim style) -> they pop and drop a pumpkin -> carry it (head stack) -> place it on your base plot -> it earns candy every second.
- 10 min: buy better tools, unlock zones, fill your base with rarer and brewed pumpkins, steal from rich bases, defend yours, fight the Blood Moon King for top tools.

## Systems
- Base: your patch (3x3 plots). Placed pumpkins earn candy/s (rarity x size x brew mutation). Pick up to sell to the Witch for a lump sum.
- Stealing: walk into another base, hold E on a pumpkin, carry it home. Defend with a Lock button (45 s, cooldown 90 s) and by bonking the thief (drops it back). Kid-safe: your best plot can't be stolen, new players shielded 10 min, max 1 robbery per victim per 2 min, victim gets 25% back.
- Tools (no pets): Rusty Sickle 5 dmg -> Wooden Scythe 15 -> Bone Scythe 50 -> Cursed Scythe 150 -> Moon Scythe 500 -> Pumpkin King Cleaver 1500 (Blood Moon drop). Buy at the tool stall; cauldron can enchant tools (+dmg/+luck). Tool Index for collection.
- Zones: Zone 1 Pumpkin Meadow (free) -> Zone 2 Graveyard (gate 1k) -> Zone 3 Haunted Woods (25k) -> Zone 4 Witch Swamp (400k). Each zone x5 value and HP.
- Cauldron: brew a carried pumpkin for x2..x10 income mutation (kept).
- Midnight: ghosts become NPC thieves that raid bases; bonk them. Blood Moon: locks off 2 min, then King raid; King drops top tools.
- Kept: Witch's Orders (rewritten), Index, Haunt + skill tree, daily chest, leaderboard, saving, shop, music, sprint, jump pads.
- Removed: seed planting and grow timers (zones replace them).

## Build order
1. First playable: breakable pumpkins + tool swing + Zone 1 + place on base + candy/s + steal + lock.
2. Tools tiers, zones 2-4 with gates, ghost thieves.
3. Blood Moon steal window + King tool drops, Orders rewrite, polish.
