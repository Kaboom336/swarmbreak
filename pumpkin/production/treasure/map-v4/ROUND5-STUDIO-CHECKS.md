# Exact Studio acceptance checks — R5

Offline art has a practical ceiling around 7/10. Static PASS does not establish Studio appearance, movement, throws or device performance. The place contains the existing isolated Keeper stand-in; real Keeper acceptance needs the actual rig and projectile controller in the owner's integration environment. This task did not edit that environment.

Open [meadow-v4.rbxl](../../prototype/meadow-v4.rbxl). Compare [overview](build-sbs.jpg), [top](build-top.jpg), [four-side underside](build-under.jpg), [main player views](build-ground.jpg) and [all 18 routes](build-route-ground.jpg). Camera coordinates and lenses are in `r5-r5final-cameras.json`: 5.5 studs above authored nominal route elevations, 24 mm perspective. Actual engine avatar-camera placement must be confirmed separately.

1. **Underside silhouette.** Orbit south, west, north, east and underneath. Confirm a wide planted lip, five irregular setbacks, two distinct hanging points and a dark lower value stack. Measured native envelope depth is 266.5 studs / 344-stud terrain width = 0.775. Confirm that remaining rectangular faces do not read as a drum and that the taper is readable at normal draw distance. Inspect the four root chains, moss drops and four intentionally floating rock accents; they are deliberate attached/floating construction, not failed prop grounding. No waterfall was added.

2. **All route floors.** Walk `summit_radial`, `spiral`, `fork_link`, `shelf`, `shelf_link`, `west`, `bridge`, `bridge_south`, `east`, `east_climb`, `east_descent`, `north_crosslink`, `south_crosslink`, `portal_north`, `ravine_ramp`, `ravine_floor_loop`, `jump_access` and `jump_recovery` in both directions. Confirm the whole 18-stud width, smooth turns/grades and unobstructed jump headroom. Inspect the retained three 12×12 jump pads and both real gaps. The static pad support tolerance is 0.75 stud; retained access/pad surface lips reach 0.441 stud. Confirm these lips do not snag, spoil jump timing or look broken.

3. **Player-eye landmarks.** Use a physical eye/camera at 5.5 studs over the actual Studio floor at every supplied camera. From the portal, confirm the large west oak, east split spires, exposed bridge and summit acorn/Keeper silhouette. From summit and every escape turn, confirm the cyan portal becomes visible in time to choose a route. The stand-in Keeper and treasure art were moved forward within the existing court; verify root grounding and objective interaction distances before integrating the real Keeper. Terrain occlusion remains a separate check from tree occlusion.

4. **Real Keeper throws.** With the actual rig, run idle → wind-up → release → projectile → recovery while players stand at the west north bend, west south bend, bridge south approach, east high court and east fork. Then move through every route. Confirm trees do not form continuous sightline screens, the animated arm/rock clears the crown silhouettes, and ballistic trajectories clear unintended foliage. Low west cover should block only the intended pockets. Four selected portal tree rays and five straight throw chords pass offline; they do not prove every position, terrain visibility or a ballistic arc.

5. **Prop feet and connected art.** Closely inspect every oak, boulder, bush, flower, lantern, banner and fence. Check for floating feet, sunk bases, cliff/edge/paving intersections and unintended joins. Static checks use a 0.20-stud contact tolerance and conservative OBB envelopes; 44 prop Wedges need visual inspection of their actual sloped faces and joins. Composite trunk/crown, stem/petal, rock/facet and post/rail interiors are intentional art connections.

6. **Fences, lamps and edges.** Verify both bridge guards have connected rails, posts at both ends and supported feet on timber edge beams; no rail intersects stone edging. Guard beams stop at the crossing, leaving the ravine exit open. Fences are cosmetic/noncollidable; confirm that this is the intended fall-risk design. Lanterns are four lights in two pairs at the portal and bridge-south junction, not rows. Confirm each pair reads as a junction and flowers read as an edge group. Black grass/path/cliff seams persisted in offline proof after cosmetic surface offsets: establish whether they exist in Studio, then fix the native overlap/export cause if they do. Do not accept the offline images as proof that this issue is solved.

7. **Traversal and recovery.** Test ordinary walking, sprint/chase if applicable, bridge warning/break, fall threshold, ravine landing, long climb-out, all jump misses, portal travel, death and respawn. Confirm routes do not become practical dead ends and the recovery camera shows the walkable ramp rather than its underside. Offline graph connectivity and 14 isolated policy assertions do not execute Roblox physics.

8. **Lighting and real device cost.** Compare Studio lighting, graphics quality low/high, shadows, portal/lantern brightness and silhouettes against the supplied references. Profile the 24,496-part map and four lights on a named real baseline phone, plus keyboard and gamepad. Record actual frame time/memory; no performance score or invented target result is claimed. Check streaming/camera behavior and input reachability in Play.

Lessons: When physical checks pass, inspect decorative construction because visible clipping can survive noncollidable parts. When measuring throws, use the actual rig and ballistic arcs because straight rays only reserve selected sightlines.
