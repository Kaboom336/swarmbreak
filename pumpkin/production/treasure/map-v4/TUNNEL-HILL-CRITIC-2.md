Lessons:

- When grass panels expose thin projecting edges, thicken and blend the terrain shoulder, because the silhouette otherwise reads as a roof.
- When a tunnel must feel carved, vary the wall and mouth profile, because repeated columns imply construction.
- When an exit should feel warm, put the warm color beyond the opening, because nearby amber props read as decoration.
