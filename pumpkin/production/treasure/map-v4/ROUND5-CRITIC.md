Planting still reads as isolated trees/rocks in empty fields despite the 17 declared 3–5-member groups. Flower edges are not visually established. West cover is partial, east orientation weak, and fences/junction cues are sparse. Black triangular path seams and edge strips across junction interiors persist. Shelf, summit-radial and jump-recovery views offer limited orientation. Exact widths, engine collision and Keeper throws cannot be established by pixels.

Disposition: retain the final measured changes and static PASS; **do not claim full visual PASS**. Stop at the owner's three-round cap.
