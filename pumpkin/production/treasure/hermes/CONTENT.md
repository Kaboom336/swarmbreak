## Game title
Steal a Treasure

## Description
Sail to five chunky islands, dodge their strange guardians, and grab a legendary treasure before somebody else does. Bring your haul home, hatch helpful blocky pets, and build the brightest treasure den on the server.

## Guardians and island mechanics

### Meadow Keeper
- Look: A straw-hat farmer in patched overalls, square boots, and a leafy green scarf; carries a blocky orchard rake.
- Personality: Warm and chatty until anyone touches the Golden Acorn, then stubbornly protective.
- Attacks:
  1. Log Roll — tosses a rolling log down the path; jump it or get knocked back.
  2. Acorn Volley — lobs three bouncing acorns with small landing shadows.
  3. Rake Sweep — makes a wide ground sweep that pushes players out of the treasure circle.
- New mechanic: Tall Grass Trail. Moving through tall grass hides the player from the guardian’s sight meter, but slows movement slightly.

### Crystal Cave guardian — Prism Warden
- Look: A broad stone golem with a faceted geode chest, uneven crystal shoulders, and glowing violet eyes.
- Personality: Quiet, precise, and convinced every loose pebble belongs exactly where it rests.
- Attacks:
  1. Crystal Line — raises a straight line of short crystals from the floor.
  2. Echo Stomp — sends a visible ring through the cave ground; jump its edge.
  3. Shard Scatter — shakes loose slow-falling crystal chunks from marked ceiling spots.
- New mechanic: Light Link. Hit or walk past glowing crystal nodes to light a route; unlit paths have false exits and slow mist.

### Frost Peak guardian — Skyrime Warden
- Look: A tall snow-armored mountaineer with an ice visor, white fur cape, and a blue-glowing climbing staff.
- Personality: Cool-headed, proud, and happy to test reckless climbers.
- Attacks:
  1. Snowball Slide — rolls a growing snowball across a ledge.
  2. Ice Lance — launches a straight icy spike from the staff.
  3. Whiteout Call — briefly fills the arena with snow, leaving only nearby silhouettes visible.
- New mechanic: Grip Meter. Sprinting and climbing drain grip; stand on marked warming stones to refill it before crossing icy walls.

### Volcano guardian — Ember Marshal
- Look: A stocky forge captain in obsidian armor, with a furnace visor and a hammer lit by orange seams.
- Personality: Loud, dramatic, and deeply serious about volcano safety rules.
- Attacks:
  1. Molten Hammer — slams the ground, creating three delayed lava circles.
  2. Cinder Burst — fires a spread of bouncing ember blocks.
  3. Ash Charge — dashes in a straight line, leaving a brief hot trail behind.
- New mechanic: Heat Gauge. The gauge rises near lava and attacks; cool off in shaded basalt alcoves or risk temporary slowed carrying.

### Ocean guardian — Reef Sentinel
- Look: A graceful armored diver with a pearl helmet, coral shoulder pads, and a trident made from pale driftwood.
- Personality: Polite, watchful, and impossible to distract for long.
- Attacks:
  1. Tide Push — sends a broad current that shoves players toward hazards.
  2. Bubble Prison — traps one area in rising bubbles; leave before they pop.
  3. Coral Spear — raises short coral spikes in a clearly marked row.
- New mechanic: Breath Bubbles. Underwater routes require collecting air bubbles; choosing longer bubble chains is safer than rushing through open water.

## Cutscene scripts

All sequences target 5–8 seconds. Keep dialogue to one readable warning bubble and use big, clear treasure silhouettes.

### Island arrival — 6 seconds
| Time | Shot and camera move | Action/audio |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | Wide shot; camera cranes up behind the boat. | Boat enters the island’s signature view; island name appears. |
| 1.5–3.5 | Camera swoops toward the treasure landmark. | A bright treasure glint points to the route. |
| 3.5–5.0 | Low tracking shot beside the player stepping off. | Pet chirps; footsteps begin. |
| 5.0–6.0 | Quick push-in to objective marker. | Banner: “Find the treasure!” |

Island dressing swaps: Meadow windmill and tall grass; Crystal Cave crystal arch; Frost Peak summit flag; Volcano lavafall; Ocean reef gate.

### Guardian wakes and warns — 6 seconds
| Time | Shot and camera move | Action/audio |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | Slow dolly toward guardian’s still silhouette. | Treasure glow wakes the guardian. |
| 1.5–3.0 | Fast tilt from feet to face. | Eyes light up; guardian straightens. |
| 3.0–4.5 | Over-the-shoulder toward the player. | Warning bubble: “Hands off my treasure!” |
| 4.5–6.0 | Camera pulls back to gameplay framing. | Attack cue sounds; boss bar appears. |

### The steal — 5 seconds
| Time | Shot and camera move | Action/audio |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | Tight orbit around the treasure pedestal. | Interact ring completes. |
| 1.0–2.5 | Snap zoom to player lifting treasure. | Big sparkle burst; treasure name appears. |
| 2.5–4.0 | Handheld-style backward tracking shot. | Guardian reacts behind the player. |
| 4.0–5.0 | Whip-pan toward exit marker. | Banner: “RUN TO THE PORTAL!” |

### Escape through the portal — 6 seconds
| Time | Shot and camera move | Action/audio |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | Side tracking shot with slight shake. | Player carries treasure past one themed hazard. |
| 2.0–3.5 | Camera races ahead and turns back on portal. | Portal opens in the path. |
| 3.5–5.0 | Rear follow shot into the portal. | Guardian’s final attack narrowly misses. |
| 5.0–6.0 | Full-screen colour flash, then fade. | Portal whoosh; escape chime. |

### Treasure opening at home — 7 seconds
| Time | Shot and camera move | Action/audio |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | Cozy wide shot; camera drifts across home display area. | Player places treasure on its stand. |
| 1.5–3.5 | Slow push-in to treasure. | It opens, cracks, or glows by treasure type. |
| 3.5–5.5 | Low angle as reward rises. | Coins and pet/reward sparkle upward. |
| 5.5–7.0 | Pull back to player and pet celebration. | Banner: “Treasure secured!” |

## Thumbnail concepts

1. Golden Acorn Grab — Meadow Keeper throws a log while a player runs with an oversized glowing acorn; use yellow treasure glow against green grass.
2. Five-Island Sprint — A diagonal map collage with every treasure floating above its island and a portal at center; use one bold “STEAL!” word.
3. Magma Orb Escape — Caldera Lion roars beside lava while the player jumps toward a blue portal holding the orange orb; strong orange-versus-cyan contrast.
4. Pet Hatch Surprise — A home treasure stand bursts open as Sunhorn Stag, Crown Geckon, Winter Wyrm, Caldera Lion, and Moonfin Narwhal lean into frame.
5. Guardian Chase — Five guardians line the background while the player and Pup race toward camera with the Pearl Clam; big readable faces and one bright portal.

## Lessons
1. When setting pet income, use rarity as the biggest jump because players must feel a clear reward for collecting harder pets.
2. When making a guardian fight for young players, telegraph every hazard with colour, shadows, or floor marks because readable danger feels fair.
3. When writing short cutscenes, show goal, threat, action, and exit in one beat because Roblox players need to return to control quickly.
