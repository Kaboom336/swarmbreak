## A — Studded Blocks

Named anchors: **Steal an Egg + Steal a Brainrot**. Cheapest conversion hypothesis. [Arrival](style-arena/studded-blocks/view1.png) · [Raid fork](style-arena/studded-blocks/view2.png) · [Summit](style-arena/studded-blocks/view3.png).

| Surface | Palette | Material / treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Ground / canopy | #7CFC3A / #49A52C | Plastic ground with coarse stud texture; SmoothPlastic cube canopy |
| Warm path | #E8B96B | SmoothPlastic matte broad strips; optional very faint stud surface |
| Wall / underside | #C8794C / #A8947A | SmoothPlastic slabs, optional low-contrast two-tone checker texture |
| Timber / rails | #80502E | SmoothPlastic beams; no wood grain needed |

**Shape language:** large rectangular terraces, straight slab walls, squared corners, two-block trees. Existing curved paths stay curved where their floors demand it; do not straighten collision to fit the art. Walls have deliberate route gaps and do not encircle/hide the floating island. Studs and checker are surface treatments, never modelled pegs or a grid of Parts.

**Whole-Meadow target prop list:** 8 trees, 3–4 ch tall with 1.5–2 ch crowns; 8 rock blocks, 0.7–2.5 ch tall; 4 bush blocks, 0.4 ch; 1 green tent, 1.5 ch high / 2 ch wide; 1 unlit fire ring, 0.3 ch; 6 rail runs, 0.8 ch high / 2–4 ch long; 2 supply groups each one barrel + crate, 0.6 ch high. Shared existing bridge, portal, nest and hero retained separately. Trees are edge frames, not a row down each lane.

**Delete/merge from current finish:** decorative petal/leaf voxels, bolts, isolated pebbles, tiny rock-chip cladding, segmented nonphysical paver trims, redundant rim blocks and chains of small lamp fixtures. Merge into broad cosmetic surfaces only after physical/tag ownership audit. Preserve actual edge rails and tunnel structural rhythm.

**Gameplay read:** cyan pad starts the route; clean ochre floor exposes raid branches and Keeper telegraphs. Farmer hat breaks against sky/quiet wall. A low stone nest puts gold above a neutral base; no white beam near the close-up. Cube grass remains below/across the sides of the 32-stud arrival apron reservation. Main risk: generic appearance plus stud shimmer; use plain path if pattern competes.

**Budget / cost:** ≤450 cosmetic BaseParts, including ≤24 mesh instances / ≤6 unique meshes / ≤18,000 placed mesh triangles; ≤6 local lights, ≤2 local shadow casters. Estimated 18–26 focused work hours, zero new asset spend; not a quote or measured result. Construction is easy, but fitting slabs over protected irregular geometry remains work.

## Lessons

1. When cleanup leaves hundreds of colours and fragmented cladding, compare a different large-shape vocabulary, because shaving a few more tiny props will not establish a coherent map style.
2. When retained collision dominates part count, separate cosmetic budgets from whole-map totals, because a cheap-looking concept cannot prove phone performance.
3. When AI concepts exaggerate routes or reward scale, keep exact gameplay geometry authoritative and prove one chosen slice, because attractive pictures can imply floors and sightlines the game does not have.
