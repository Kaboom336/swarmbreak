# Candidate: quarry chase

Review fun, fairness and whether each event reads in a muted 10-second vertical clip. Compare guarded-objective clarity to Steal an Egg and anticipation/consequence to Catastrophic Horse Racing (existing project reference bars, not newly tested games).

One geode, one guardian, 4-8 players. Same-item 60-second deadline starts after a 2-second steal; portal immediately usable; drop/handoff never renews deadline. No hiding or body blocking. Carry speed assumed 16 studs/s. Proposed island 520 x 400, NE camp +48, west shoulder +24, mine pit -16, cyan exit SW at 0. Camp has a clear SW-facing viewing apron: main route left, cart central, cave mouth right; silhouettes and exit beacon visible. Crystal crowns behind camp, never in this viewing cone.

Main permanent north/west switchback ~29-32 seconds; optional skylit cave east/south ~33-36 seconds; cart shortcut ~18-22 seconds. Estimates only. Three separate safety approaches. Two cross-links and permanent 18-wide walking track bypass. Ramps <=25 degrees, turns 28 wide; cave floor 18 wide, headroom 24, three skylights, two ends and side passing room. Guardian pursues inside using a 5-wide local tail sweep; no dynamite beneath roof. No forced choke on main.

Five features:
1. Two TNT barrels on a widened track court. Players or guardian ignite via readable interaction/throw. 1-second spark plus 10-stud ring. Blast knocks exposed geode loose into reachable socket, harmless voxel debris, brief shake, nonblocking crater. Step outside ring or emergency dismount to bypass; existing mercy respected. 15-second per-barrel cooldown. Portrait trailing camera keeps spark, rival, dropped geode and crater centered.
2. Support TNT: 1-second fuse then 2-second amber cracks before track breaks. 16-stud gap over mine pit. Fixed launch ramp, broad landing. Full-speed 24-stud/s cart jumps; below-speed braking cart drops into pit; 18-wide climb-out adds ~8-10 seconds. Dismount before marker onto permanent bypass. One switch of state per 20 seconds, repair only when empty. Low side camera sees support, launch, landing and pit. No guardian attacks during committed flight.
3. Guardian throws one dynamite stick: 1.5-second raised-arm tell then fixed ring and 1-second fuse; radius 8 studs, no homing. Exit ring or use rock. 7-second cooldown, no roof casts. Camera over carrier shoulder includes bright fuse/ring and guardian above.
4. Cavern crystals: hit first cluster, then three clusters ripple with visible growing white cracks and 0.8-second spacing. Each 6-stud ring warns 1 second before burst; shards do not damage, local pressure wave knocks loose geode if hit. An 18-wide center lane stays untouched. 20-second reset, only empty. Camera along cave axis holds glowing fuse-like wave, runner, loose geode, skylight.
5. Optional switch lever on exposed court: rivals route next cart into 24-wide dead-end padded bumper. 2-second amber arrow warning; driver can override at approach or dismount to walk. Low-speed stop, no injury/lock-in, return path always open, adds ~3-4 seconds. 12-second cooldown; committed carts unaffected. Rear three-quarter camera sees lever pull, arrow, rival cart, soft bonk.

Global guard: one damaging event per local court at a time, 3-second post-hit mercy, no deadline refresh, reachable item sockets. Cosmetic debris never collides or obscures warnings; crater is a visual decal/recess with unchanged walk collider. Mild shake opt-out. No forced camera moves. Cartoon TNT, no realistic injury. Big air and chain shatter are the two hero clips, each setup 0-3s, payoff 3-6s, reaction/recovery 6-10s.
