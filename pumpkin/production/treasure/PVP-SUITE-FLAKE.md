## Recurrence investigation / pvp2 — Codex, 2026-10-17

**Reproduced cause: Steal retries reuse a displaced actor position.** The `limit=9` diagnostic identifies **Steal**, not Grab (limit=4). Socket `(0,124,-456)` is the summit nest; the actual handoff court is `(0,76,-402)`. `hold` accepted `missing/short hold` after a guardian interruption, then immediately began another hold without approaching again. Production `hit` clears the hold and applies up to 5 studs of knockback. A valid first attempt at root `(3.3305,127.56,-462.2564)` followed by a hit from Keeper `(0,129.5,-456)` leaves root `(5.68,127.56,-466.67)` and deterministically refuses the second begin at **12.262 studs**, blocker NONE. The item remains **Nest** throughout this refusal; no dropped-item settling is involved.

**Evidence and limits:** `pvp2-repro.luau` executes the archived runner's actual `driver`/`hold`, production `hit`/range check and authoritative LootRules. The inverse fixture above is derived from the reported endpoint minus production knockback; it is **not a measured earlier-suite starting state**. The 864-case cross product varies eight seed/clock phases, Keeper Guard/Chase state and three positions, mercy 0/3 seconds, three actor offsets and three drop-rest inputs. Before: **144 refusals**; fixed: **zero**. A protected hit, inactive Keeper or a hit direction that leaves the actor within range does not produce this refusal. Changing drop-rest input does not affect this Nest-stage failure. Seeds vary clock phase, not an emulated physics RNG. Collision-free Blockcast/sight and kinematic Humanoid movement are injected; the production 5-stud displacement and item transitions execute unchanged. This proves a deterministic runner bug, not which exact hit occurred in the owner's live run.

**Likely live stage (inferred):** PvP's initial dummy Steal runs with the guardian paused, but `Test.pvp` later unpauses it and calls `expiryTest`, which approaches the summit and uses the same unsafe Steal retry loop. That final expiry Steal can therefore fail the whole PvP suite after a successful handoff. No live substage trace was supplied, so attribution to that invocation remains inferred. Full StudioPassAll recurrence rate and engine physics/navmesh are **UNKNOWN** here; no Studio Play was run.

**Fix:** Before every Steal begin, including every existing retry, walk/navigate to a floor-validated point at the **current active item's position**, then re-read the active item and root. Require distance <=4 in 3D before acting (inside the unchanged production Steal limit of 9); this accommodates the driver's existing 3-stud horizontal arrival tolerance plus root height. Cancel the previous MoveTo destination when stopping. An 8-second close-approach guard bounds the new setup; all existing hold durations, retry counts, cargo deadlines, gameplay limits and assertions remain unchanged. Apply the shared fix to loop Steal, initial PvP dummy Steal and final expiry Steal. Create the dummy's movement driver before its first hold and reuse it for the existing route. No gameplay or geometry change.

When diagnosing a shared hold failure, use the action's limit and socket coordinates to identify the stage before investigating dropped-item geometry.
