**Decision: do not lock silhouette for dressing yet.** The enclosed shortcut reads in the entry and reverse views, and the lantern sequence gives useful depth. The largest remaining issues affect route reading rather than decoration.

## Exactly three biggest visual gaps

1. **The supports read as a narrow rectangular cage rather than repeated mine arches.** Entry and reverse show long hanging vertical timber ends and closely stacked flat crossbars. The target has complete, legible support frames with angled shoulders and a stronger overhead rock envelope. Establish clearer cosmetic frame silhouettes and a more deliberate ceiling rhythm within the fixed collision envelope. The central sightline is visible, but the frame ends visually crowd it.

2. **The warm walking track loses its continuous shape.** Entry has a dark foreground, a strong transverse threshold and two competing floor strips; middle becomes a broad brown junction with scattered slabs. In the target, the warm ground and edge stones carry one readable route into depth and around the branch. Strengthen the continuous floor ribbon and its edges through the junction so the safe route reads before individual slabs or green seams.

3. **The two destination exits are not yet readable as a deliberate pair.** Far shows an oversized rectangular opening under heavy horizontal bars; middle reveals open daylight but does not clearly explain the bent branch. The cutaway helps infer the layout, but the player views do not establish two distinct destinations. Give each portal a recognizable framed silhouette, and make the branch's turning mouth legible without exposing the whole destination around the bend. Keep the player-height view clear of low-looking crossbars.

## Honest unknowns

- These stills cannot verify collision clearance, camera clipping or traversal. No player avatar is present to establish actual headroom.
- The overview is explicitly a cutaway, so its missing roof is not evidence of an open gameplay ceiling.
- The bent destination's actual visibility while moving is unknown; the supplied player views do not prove it reads clearly.
- Warmth is assessed only as route hierarchy here. Materials, detail density and final lighting quality are outside this silhouette round.

Lesson: when a route bends, establish the turning mouth in the player's view, because an overhead layout alone cannot communicate the destination.
