# Evidence limits

Four locked cameras in CAMERAS.json: spiral entry, mid-spiral up, shelf edge down,
overview. The overview was corrected to the northwest to expose the spiral side,
then rerendered identically for BEFORE/R1/R2; the three player cameras stay exact.
Add MeadowOrigin (0,76,-360) for canonical world positions. All player
eyes checked outside collision/query solids. No cutaway or camera move per round.
Renders reconstruct each mounted Builder with native geometry; material texture,
SurfaceGui font and PointLight shading are offline proxies. Existing kit swaps
remain native proxies; deferred S5 rock meshes are absent from these images but
explicitly included in final production DropSample. S6 adds no imported mesh.

TARGET.png is generated from these three player-camera views with built-in
imagegen, never a schematic. It is aspiration, not a geometry approval. Matching
warm paving and edge stones is a preservation requirement, not visual acceptance.
The new low shoulder studies may remain occluded in player views; an overview or
instance count does not establish readable continuous descent or summit exposure.
Protected box walls and skyline remain visual misses under the solid freeze.

The contact audit uses unchanged .035-stud tolerance, OBB/SAT penetration,
physical-world support graph, one-stud lane buffer and seven-stud headroom.
A separate projected oriented-box check reserves one stud at *every altitude*.
Earlier conservative axis-aligned broadphase false positives were resolved with
oriented SAT, without weakening the distance. One real R1 sill penetration was
fixed by moving the study to a different supported shoulder within the same gap.
A trial closer/longer entry-lamp placement failed the lane margin and was rejected;
the original measured safe wall positions remain, with limited camera visibility.

Process deviation: R1 source and its gaps were written after the target existed,
but PRE-R1 board assembly failed in two local image-library attempts and completed
during R1 compilation. Its unchanged BEFORE renders and target predate the edits;
do not claim the composed board itself predated source editing. PRE-R2 was saved
from final R1 renders before any R2 source edits. No extra visual round is counted.
