## Progression risk

The first durable bank is the critical retention event. X offers the clearest path to it, but one item per zone with refill only after resolution can make seven of eight arrivals wait on the first player's entire attempt. A player cannot progress to zone 2 until banking zone 1. Thus the proposed eight-player opening can measure access to a pickup instead of learning, and the first-Coin target is unproven. Rival recovery could also extend another player's wait under the original deadline. No exact queue time is claimed.

The doubling income ladder gives a legible reason to push forward, but may quickly make safe earlier banks feel negligible. Boots can soften the learning curve, yet their purchase timing cannot be inferred without egg opening, existing income timing, and actual run results. The five-minute boots/zone-3 target remains UNKNOWN.

## Failure and practice risk

Banked progress staying safe, a short empty-handed retry, locked aim, visible tells, and a walking alternative are strong foundations. Difficulty quality depends on actual layouts, speeds, camera view, and server overlap; the numerical success ladder cannot establish fairness.

Five prior completions is a useful operational definition of practiced, but selects for players who persisted and succeeded. It does not show whether struggling newcomers learned or left. Count attempts and failed attempts before those completions as well. Early pickup denial must be recorded separately from losing cargo during a real attempt.

X's primary practice risk is repeated straight returns becoming routine before its timing demands become interesting. Y adds navigation that can obscure the lesson; Z adds ordering and shared-spoke interference that can make failure attribution harder. Keep each zone's new demand recognizable and preserve the stated maximum of two learned demands.

## Smallest correction

**Resolve the first-zone supply rule before expanding beyond five zones.** Change only zone 1 so its source refills three seconds after pickup, independently of the carried item's eventual resolution. Keep one available source item at a time; track each live cargo's existing identity and original deadline separately. This is a proposed rule change and additional lifecycle work, not an assertion that the code supports it. It preserves the shared map and eight-player proposal while reducing a mandatory opening queue. If concurrent zone-1 cargo cannot fit the intended implementation, the eight-player opening remains an unresolved shipping blocker.

Then validate the full eight-player opening and solo five-zone loop before adding themes or zones. Observe time to first bank and first earned Coin, pickup denials, attempts per successful bank, and whether players voluntarily retry a failure. No numerical pass threshold beyond the pack's existing targets is invented here.

Lesson: When every player's progression requires the same first pickup, test simultaneous arrivals because a readable solo tutorial can become a queue in multiplayer.
