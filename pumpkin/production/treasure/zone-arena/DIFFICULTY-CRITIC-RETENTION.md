## What makes the difficulty meaningful

The new demand plus at most two learned demands is stronger than piling on fifteen hazards. Locked attack commitments, previewable routes, unchanged warning time, recovery mercy, and walking alternatives let failure teach a repeatable decision. The alternate walking route must still test timing; otherwise a difficult vault becomes a cosmetic shortcut. Zone 14's solo fallback is essential because rival availability cannot define progression difficulty.

The INFERRED new/practiced target gap suggests mastery could matter: zone 8 targets 40% versus 74%, and zone 15 targets 10% versus 46%. These are hypotheses. Do not tune solely to the percentage: a low success rate caused by unreadable tells, server delay, blocked claims, or exhausted raid slots is failure of the design, not successful difficulty. Exclude technical and queue failures when judging the intended challenge, while still reporting them separately.

## Reward, failure cost, and upgrades

Doubling common income each zone creates a legible reason to risk the next zone. Banked income surviving failure protects progression; local retry avoids a second outbound commute. At a hypothetical independent 10% success probability, expected attempts are ten, but actual attempts are correlated through learning, fatigue, gear, and contention. Never present that arithmetic as observed session length.

Permit and boots costs rise alongside common income, so the exponential numbers alone do not prove improving pacing. Existing passive income could also let a stuck player wait out purchases. Mandatory timing decisions must remain after buying boots, shield, or decoy. Optional high-zone mastery and collection are healthier than mandatory rebirth that erases earned access.

**Strongest objection:** X can make late failure expensive through return duration while passive home income and eight-pet replacement remain insufficiently specified. A player may rationally wait for money or repeat a safe zone instead of practicing the exciting one.

**Required correction before expanding beyond zones 1–3:** define eight-slot replacement as a deliberate safe-home exchange, preserve the existing pet until the replacement bank is durably confirmed, show the actual income increase, and retain failed-run income protection. Record full attempt duration and cause of failure in the first slice. Before tuning zones 7–15, compare expected useful income per active minute against repeating the prior comfortable zone; use measured attempts and time, not success targets alone. If late-zone practice is dominated, adjust reward or non-travel failure friction while preserving the timing test. Do not add an instant cargo bank.

## Evidence needed before accepting retention claims

Measure first successful bank, first upgrade, next-zone voluntary attempt, failed-attempt retry, abandonment after repeated failures, and performance by novice versus practiced players at matched gear. Capture queue time, technical failures, and each threat separately. First validate a meaningful learnable challenge in zones 1–3, then test zone 8 and zone 15 challenge prototypes before making all intermediate decoration. All retention predictions above remain INFERRED until these observations exist.
