# Steal a Treasure — phone review · 2026-10-18
Diagnosis: We added rules and threats faster than interesting things the thief can do.  
Diagnosis: Dodge → walk home → pet/Coins spreads payoff across chores; Zion says NOT FUN.  
Diagnosis: We need a treasure toy, player-caused surprises and a haul worth showing; fun remains untested.  

1. Screaming Diamond: toss a name-shouting gem to redirect the guardian; relay, betray and steal back.  
2. Steal the King's Bed: steal the sleeping king with his bed; surf it out alone or with friends.  
3. Trojan Treasure: become a gold statue, get carried into a rival vault, pop out and rob it.  
4. Roll Up the Vault: grow a sticky gold ball by stealing coins, furniture and rivals' loose loot.  
5. Granny's Golden Teeth: steal wearable dentures; whistle her dog into a slapstick getaway.  
6. Cannonball Burglars: fire yourself in a chest, rob the vault and bounce the loot home.  

TOP TWO: Roll Up the Vault + Steal the King's Bed; choose ONE later proof, not a mash-up.  
Three independent simulated critics ranked fun + standout; no real players tested. Trojan: freshest, but waiting risk.
