# Pumpkin Patch Panic: your launch steps

Do these in order. Code work is done; these need your account.

## 1. Account
- Turn on 2-Step Verification (Settings > Security).

## 2. Publish
- Studio: File > Publish to Roblox. Name: **Pumpkin Patch Panic 🎃**. Devices: all.
- Game Settings > Security: turn ON "Enable Studio Access to API Services" (saving needs it).
- Place settings: Max players **16** (the map has 24 patches; 16 keeps servers busy).
- Keep it **Private** for the first test.

## 3. Game page (Creator Dashboard > your game > Configure)
- Fill the Maturity & Compliance questionnaire (cartoon ghosts, no blood, no violence beyond bonking: should come out Mild).
- Genre: Simulation (Incremental Simulator). You can only change it every 3 months.
- Icon: `launch-art/icon-draft.png` (512x512). A drawn icon with the game name would do better later.
- Thumbnails: `launch-art/thumb-patches.jpg` and `launch-art/thumb-king.jpg` (1920x1080).
- Description (copy this):

> Grow spooky pumpkins in YOUR patch, brew them in the Magic Cauldron for x2 to x10 candy, and sell to the Witch! 🎃
> 👻 At midnight, ghosts come to steal your pumpkins. BONK them!
> 🩸 Sell enough and the Blood Moon wakes the PUMPKIN KING. Team up to beat him!
> 🌱 10 seeds · 📖 50-pumpkin Index · ☠ Haunt rebirth + Soul skill tree · 🎁 daily chest · 🧹 Witch's Orders
> Halloween event, new seeds coming!

## 5. First players
- Make the game **Limited**, invite friends or a Roblox group to play for 15 minutes, and watch.
- Post 10-20 second clips (Blood Moon King fight, Cursed brew) on TikTok/Shorts with the game name.
- Check Dashboard > Analytics weekly: first-play bounce, D1 and D7 retention, onboarding funnel (the game logs it).
- Do not spend on ads until people come back on day 2.
