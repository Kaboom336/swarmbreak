# R20 plan (arena r19 winner, merged from 2 blind judges) — Steal a Pumpkin

Rule a kid learns: SMASH orange pumpkins for CANDY 🍬. STEAL glowing "?" pumpkins for PETS 🐾. Candy buys scythes; a better scythe wins steal fights.

## Build list (in order)
1. Split the verbs. Wild (farmed) pumpkins burst into candy that flies to the player; they are never carried or placed. Only nest (mystery) pumpkins are carried, placed and hatch into pets. Floating icon over each wild pumpkin "🍬" and over each nest "🐾 PETS". First smash toast "Smash = CANDY!", first grab toast "Steal = PETS!". Old saves: carried farmed pumpkins convert to candy on join; existing placed pets stay.
2. Farm depth (per zone 1..7): small pumpkin HP 10/30/90/270/810/2400/7300, candy 1/3/9/27/80/240/720; big pumpkin x6 HP and x6 candy (25% of spawns); Golden 1 in 40, x20 candy, sparkly. 40 pumpkins per zone, respawn 8 s. Scythes damage 5/15/40/110/300/800/2200, cost 0/150/1.2k/9k/60k/400k/2.5M (names Rusty, Iron, Bone, Witch, Vampire, Candy, King). Crit 10% x3 (orange number). Tier 3+ swing hits up to 3 pumpkins in a 6-stud arc.
3. Arrows: one target only. Ground chevrons (already big) red; GREEN while carrying a stolen pumpkin and pointing home. A 6-stud bobbing red arrow over the target, visible through walls (AlwaysOnTop billboard). An off-screen edge pointer (red triangle on the screen edge) when the target is behind the camera. Banner 3-4 words.
4. Scripted first minutes (tutorial 5 steps): 1 "Smash pumpkins!" (collect 30 candy) → 2 "Buy the Iron Scythe!" (150 → make first step pay ~50 per minute; tune so it takes ~45-60 s) → 3 "Steal a glowing pumpkin!" (Zone1 nest) → 4 "Place it in your pen!" (hatch) → 5 "Fill your pen 2/6". Then "Unlock the Graveyard!". Banner title ALWAYS matches the current order verb (bug: "Smash a Pumpkin!" shown for "Sell 10 pumpkins").
5. Pens full: when carrying a mystery pumpkin and all pedestals are full, banner "Pens full! Swap or Sell"; stepping into your pen offers two prompts: "Swap weakest pet" (weakest goes to Sell value) or "Sell for X 🍬". Sell stall unlocks the first time pens fill (not at 30 min). Sell = 50% of that pet's 1-hour earnings.
6. Steal beat: hold E 1.0 s to grab. Wake cutscene 1.2 s, skippable, letterbox bars, camera to guard face, eyes glow, "!" + roar, then "RUN!!" — full only on the first steal per zone, later a 0.4 s shake. When the guard catches you: a 3 s "FIGHT!" tap/click bar (one big button; mobile friendly). Fill rate base 40% win chance feel, +15% per scythe tier above the zone index, max 90%. Win = guard stunned 3 s (stars), you keep running. Lose = BONKED, pumpkin returns to nest. One fight per steal.
7. Pet sizes like Steal an Egg: base height by rarity — Common 3.5 studs, Rare 5, Epic 7, Legendary 9, Mythic 12; each hatch rolls a weight (kg) and scales height by (kg/median)^(1/4) clamped 0.8-1.6; show "12.4 kg" on the pet tag and the reveal card. Pen pedestal fits any size (pet stands on top).
8. Clutter fixes: max ONE big centre message at a time (queue them; midnight reset + raid + got-away were stacked); gate "UNLOCKED ✓" world billboard 50% smaller and hidden beyond 80 studs; "Free base" sign smaller (MaxDistance 100).
9. World 2 teaser: a locked glowing portal at the far end of Zone7 "🌨 WORLD 2: FROST FIELDS — coming soon". Zone data stays in one Config table so a reskin is a table swap.

Later (not now): pet followers that auto-smash, feeding duplicates, shared Giant Pumpkin event, World 2 itself, Robux items (Golden Scythe, auto-win fights pass, instant hatch).
