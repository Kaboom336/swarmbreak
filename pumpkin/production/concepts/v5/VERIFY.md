## Buildability conclusion
The richness can come from layered modular architecture, low-poly MeshParts, native material contrast and direct/local lighting. v4's hair-thin straw, micro foliage, painted bounce light and illustrated blur are replaced/cut in the construction plan. A buildable equivalent is not a promise of pixel-identical rendering. The next proof is an actual Studio hub capture and baseline-phone profile using the documented material/part/light caps, before expanding the content.

## Lessons
- When a sharpening edit loses essential UI, select the stronger action frame and name its remaining artifact because the three-try cap is not permission to claim perfection.
- When preserving richness in Roblox, keep layered silhouettes and local light because painted microdetail cannot establish a usable asset budget.
