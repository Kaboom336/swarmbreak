# Independent blind judgment: Steal a Pumpkin v4

**Decision: none of the 15 screens clears 8/10.** Best: panels-index, 7.5. Weakest: panels-shop-index, 6. These are polished concept illustrations, but they do not yet convincingly pass as screenshots of a popular playable Roblox game.

## Evidence and calibration

- Observed: inspected all 15 eligible v4 PNG originals at native resolution and the selected reference originals; compared their camera, geometry/material language, interaction state, UI hierarchy and composition. Read REFS3.md. Artist scores were not opened or used.
- Comparison JPEGs place ours at 1280 pixels wide above 2-3 uncropped reference frames in one labelled row. Those review JPEGs are scaled; full-size source inspection preceded scoring.
- Upload dates and game attribution are **local-ledger reported**, copied from REFS3.md; not independently reverified online. Dates mean video upload date, not update date or capture date. Chart position today, actual click-through and our runtime/device performance are **UNKNOWN**.
- Scale: 10 = indistinguishable from a top-20 game screenshot; 8 = plausibly a real popular game worth clicking; 6 = visibly AI/amateur concept; 4 = programmer art. Half-points are subjective judgments, not measurements or predicted player ratings.
- Detailed attractive art does not automatically earn screenshot credibility. Simple reference geometry is not a defect by itself. Compression, creator overlays and lower intrinsic mobile resolution are not counted as faults in either game''s art. BGSI clutter is a counterexample, not a target to copy.
- There is no literal mass hatch, pre-boss lobby, exact matching zone biome or first-ever-player onboarding reference in this set. Reveal frames are acquisition/chest/catch analogues; RIVALS is a combat analogue, not a second boss. These limitations reduce comparison specificity; no timing or gameplay claims are inferred.

## Per-screen judgment

### first-5-min — 6.5/10

Compared: `first-minutes-adopt-me-1.jpg`, `first-minutes-doors-1.jpg`. Best fit in the supplied set: **Adopt Me!**.

- The huge foreground pursuer and low cinematic camera make a chase poster; Adopt Me keeps the controlled character and interactable in a usable view.
- Home is a building label and scattered arrows, with no precise deposit interaction or arrival state; the reference pins its prompt to the actual object.
- A disabled STEAL button occupies the main action position while carrying; the picture explains danger better than the next usable action.

**Fix:** Use a normal follow camera and one visible deposit target with a contextual carry/deposit state.

[Labelled comparison](first-5-min-cmp.jpg)

### guards-and-king-fight — 7.0/10

Compared: `boss-fight-deepwoken-1.jpg`, `boss-fight-rivals-1.jpg`. Best fit in the supplied set: **Deepwoken**.

- The king, player and impact are readable, but the low hero camera and detailed warm set still look like a promotional render rather than a live combat view.
- Orange danger rings merge with the gold floor decoration; RIVALS distinguishes incoming damage from outgoing attack through a different shape and colour.
- The scythe arc and king impact overlap in one bright knot; the still does not clearly identify which attack is hitting which participant.

**Fix:** Raise the gameplay camera, quiet the floor pattern and separate the boss impact, player swing and danger zone.

[Labelled comparison](guards-and-king-fight-cmp.jpg)

## Lessons

- When judging screenshot credibility, compare camera and interaction state before decorative detail, because attractive illustrations can conceal incomplete gameplay presentation.
- When a screen shows placeholders or an ambiguous primary action, resolve one concrete state before polishing its frame, because more ornament does not make the action usable.
- When evaluating a zone strip or mass reveal without a literal matching reference, name the analogue and its limit, because composition evidence cannot establish runtime behaviour.
