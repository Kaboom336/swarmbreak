## Lessons

- When a generator invents egg stalls or pumpkin faces, explicitly remove them in the next pass, because visual genre defaults can replace our mechanics.
- When revising a batch, pass the selected shared model/tool frame again, because text constraints alone do not prevent anatomical drift.
- When showing action, require target, physical contact or carried reward, and destination in-frame, because a headline does not prove a playable moment.
