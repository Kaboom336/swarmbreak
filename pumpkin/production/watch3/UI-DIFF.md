## 12 fixes with the biggest visual impact, ordered

1. Thick black text stroke everywhere: max(2 px, 0.006h) round join (currently about 0.0028h).
2. Fix the currency counter: on-screen icon 0.058h, number 0.058h Heavy Italic #FFC933, green "+" chip, no clipping at the left edge.
3. Remove the giant world billboard letters ("OP", "SELL") that clip into the HUD corners (MaxDistance about 60 studs).
4. Black 2.5 px outline on every button and card, plus a 0.012h dark lip and a hard shadow.
5. Render HUD icons as 3D models with black outline clones (replace flat glyphs); icons at 80% of tile.
6. Add the stud texture and diagonal gloss shine to panel bodies and headers.
7. Make price buttons colourful (orange when affordable) with a bold white number; grey only when disabled.
8. Strip boxes from the objective and timer chips; stroked floating text, 0.034-0.036h.
9. Scale scythe art to 62% of the card with a glow plus rarity border; add tilted NEXT/BEST ribbons.
10. Bold close X: 0.036h glyph, outline, lip, inside the header with a margin.
11. Uniform margins and gaps (0.012h) and larger labels (0.034h buttons, 0.058h titles).
12. Tactile motion and sound: press squash with face drop, hover brightness, Back overshoot open, HUD slide-out, a click/whoosh/coin sound on every control.
