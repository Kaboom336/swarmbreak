# Arena round 9: gameplay rework (2026-10-08)

Trigger: Zion said the pyramid goal has no purpose, the cauldron is unclear, farming is boring.

## Proposals (scratchpad arena/r9/PA-PD.md)
- PA Spooky Patch: own plot, plant seeds by rarity, weather mutations, sell to witch (Grow a Garden).
- PB Steal a Jack-o'-Lantern: yard of candy-making jacks, steal from neighbors, gate locks (Steal a Brainrot).
- PC Keep the Lanterns Lit: co-op, place lanterns, ghosts at midnight, survive nights (99 Nights).
- PD Pumpkin Smash: mallet breakables, HP by size, Pumpkin King boss (Pet Sim breakables).

## Blind judges

A four-way tie: each idea wins one lens and loses another. So the verdict is a hybrid that takes each idea's winning piece and drops its losing piece.

## Verdict: "Pumpkin Patch Panic" (hybrid)
- Own it (PA, J3): each player claims a patch, plants seeds of different rarity, sells for candy, buys better seeds. Progress to keep and come back to.
- Fun hit (PD, J2): harvesting and defending uses one satisfying mallet BONK.
- Halloween threat without PvP exploits (PC + PB, J1): at midnight, ghosts drift from the graveyard to steal pumpkins from patches; bonk them away. Solo-friendly, kid-safe, no player stealing.
- Cauldron purpose: drop a pumpkin in to brew a random spooky mutation (Glowing, Ghostly, Candy) that multiplies sell price.
- Giant Jack purpose: every sale on the server fills it; when full, Blood Moon (mutation chance up) and the Pumpkin King boss everyone bonks together.
- Removed: pyramid slam, tier quotas, Strength gate.
- Cut for risk: trading, player stealing, offline growth (until saving works after publishing).
