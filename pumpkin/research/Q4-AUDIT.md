# Q4-AUDIT: Pumpkin game-feel and quality audit

Audited 2026-10-05 against the entire `pumpkin/` Rojo project, its tests, the shipped captures in `pumpkin/art/`, and [Q4-REFERENCES.md](Q4-REFERENCES.md). The audit is a pre-change diagnosis: it does not claim that an old capture overrides newer source configuration. In particular, the night screenshots show the shipped presentation that prompted the playtest, while `default.project.json` now specifies daytime lighting.

Reference shorthand used below:

- **Stone** — +1 Stone Skipping: immediate `+1` event feedback and an obvious progression chain ([Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/111543903102439/1-Stone-Skipping), [gameplay guide](https://stoneskipping.wiki/)).
- **Egg** — Break and Steal an Egg: commit → danger → return → bank loop, bright result presentation ([Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/114326934417838/Break-and-Steal-an-Egg), [gameplay video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uT5R6PyMrQg)).
- **Fat Roll** — Get Fat And Roll Race: visible body-scale progression and downhill payoff ([Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/14494334042/Get-Fat-And-Roll-Race), [gameplay video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4-jSDYpBANo)).
- **Box** — Ride a Box Down a Slide: readable contained rider, ramps, rocks, balance, and speed ([Roblox variant](https://www.roblox.com/games/14777367640/Ride-A-Box-Down-A-Slide), [gameplay video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pm0uwYs2Nzw)).
- **Marble** — Mega Marble Run Pit: avatar inside the marble; steep drops, funnels, walls, curved guards, and a tunnel ([Fandom course notes](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:SeanMichaell/Mega_Marble_Run_Pit), [gameplay video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6PBkz9gq6c)).
- **Roblox guidance** — the platform targets 60 FPS (16.67 ms/frame), recommends a real low-end baseline device and streaming for larger places, and advises contextual, visual onboarding with delayed hints ([performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization), [design for performance](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/design), [onboarding techniques](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques)).

Severity means: **P1** blocks the core fantasy, comprehension, or a fair playable run; **P2** materially weakens quality, retention, or accessibility; **P3** is polish. Each row has exactly one implementation owner. Q4-E owns every important item outside the narrower A–D briefs.

## Second-pass themes: kid appeal and the 2025-2026 hit bar

This deeper pass uses the current Roblox chart as the live snapshot and the breakout set identified by Roblox and Newzoo: **Grow a Garden**, **Steal a Brainrot**, and **99 Nights in the Forest** all launched in 2025, while Roblox reported that Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, and Dead Rails were three of its top ten experiences by Robux spend in June 2025 ([Roblox chart](https://www.roblox.com/charts?nl=true&nu=true), [Roblox Q2 2025 shareholder letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2025/q2/Q2-25-SH-Letter.pdf), [Newzoo 2025 review](https://newzoo.com/articles/top-roblox-experiences-2025)). The lesson is not to clone one game's art. Their looks differ sharply. The shared bar is an instantly understandable fantasy, large collectible silhouettes, a short verb loop, visible state change, and frequent reasons to return.

- **Look:** Grow a Garden uses bright block colour, oversized crops, glowing mutations, and simple fenced plots; Steal a Brainrot builds recognition around absurd, high-contrast collectible characters; 99 Nights uses a darker forest and campfire focal point without losing readable tools and threats ([Grow a Garden gameplay image](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/grow-garden-thunderstorm-time), [Steal a Brainrot overview](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sim/one-of-robloxs-biggest-experiences-right-now-is-a-bizarre-italian-brainrot-character-stealing-simulator-and-lord-help-me-i-now-understand-why-its-so-popular/), [99 Nights gameplay image](https://bo3.gg/fr/games/articles/difficulty-and-horror-level-review-of-99-nights-in-the-forest)). Pumpkin's current shipped shots are saturated but flat: repeated slabs, weak material separation, and an orange sphere/mesh do not yet form an authored toy world.
- **Juice and feel:** the hit pattern is a visible transformation or acquisition on nearly every useful interaction - plant/harvest/mutation, buy/steal/bank, gather/build/survive - with the valuable result remaining in the world as a collection or base upgrade. Pumpkin has some wall debris, shockwaves, flying coins, text pops, and pet sparkle, but no coherent effect language spanning grow, launch, steer, pickup, dodge, hit, hatch, equip, unlock, and cash-out.
- **Fun movement:** Pumpkin's ride can be its advantage over mostly stationary incremental hits, but only if rolling, lane changes, bumps, airtime, landings, and the rider's pose are playful in themselves. Translation plus UI numbers is not enough; the vehicle must wobble, squash, bank, trail, react, and land while controls stay predictable.
- **Monetization:** current hit pricing provides anchors, not a mandate. Steal a Brainrot's tracked offers include a permanent **2x Money pass at 299 Robux**, **VIP at 375 Robux**, a **Blackhole Starter Pack at 189 Robux**, and a repeatable **2x server-luck boost for 15 minutes at 249 Robux** ([live pass tracker](https://www.rolimons.com/game/109983668079237), [shop snapshot](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Shop)). Grow a Garden's Forever Pack documents an escalating sequence from **39 to 1,199 Robux**, while a dated event seed pack cost **199 Robux** ([Forever Pack](https://growagarden.fandom.com/wiki/Limited_Time_Shop), [Zen pack, July 2025](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/grow-a-garden-hinomai-seeds/)). These are dated public snapshots and prices can vary; Roblox explicitly supports repeatable developer products and personalized pricing, so Pumpkin must query the price shown to the player rather than hard-code it ([Roblox developer products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products)).
- **Retention:** Grow a Garden advertises offline growth and rotating stock; Steal a Brainrot advertises income, stealing, and rebirth; 99 Nights turns an easily stated survival target into escalating session progress ([Grow a Garden](https://www.roblox.com/games/126884695634066/Grow-a-Garden), [Steal a Brainrot](https://www.rolimons.com/game/109983668079237), [99 Nights](https://www.roblox.com/games/79546208627805/99-Nights-in-the-Forest)). Pumpkin needs the same clarity at two scales: a satisfying 10-14 second ride now, and a visible next return goal (egg, zone, rotating challenge, cosmetic, or authored event) later. Retention should add novelty and collection, not timer clutter before the first fun run.
