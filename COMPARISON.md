### Design gauntlet, round 1 (2026-09-30, three judges: loop, money, first session; four items built and reviewed)

What the judges said we already do as well as the niche leaders:
- Time to action: a player is fighting 3 s after the loop starts (Config.WaveStartDelay), no menus in the way; this matches the one-sentence loop the chart leaders share (RESEARCH.md section 1).
- Boss cadence and presentation: a boss every 5 waves with a 4 s intro card, a boss health bar, summons (Queen hatches Runners), and a three-boss cycle in infinite mode (Enemies.bossForWave).
- Weapon variety with roles: 15 weapons across Hitscan / Melee / Orbital / Beam and 4 rarities, sorted on a hotbar, held models per weapon. Final Swarm's DPS vs AoE split is covered.
- Persistent progression is real and tested: Gems (Evolutions.gemsForRun rewards deeper runs and a win), four element shards from specific bosses and every 5th wave, one evolution path per weapon with 3 tiers and combat effects (heal on kill, chain, lifesteal, pull). ProfileSchema.sanitize drops junk and has tests.
- Crate odds are printed right under the Open crate button and Weapons.RarityOdds is the single source (test 'rarity odds sum to 100' guards it), so the paid-random-item policy is met by construction.
- The client never names a product id: Shop.server's Prompt handler maps a key to Monetization and refuses id 0 with 'Not for sale yet', so the game is safe to publish before the dashboard items exist.
- ProcessReceipt grants coins, opens the crate or revives before returning PurchaseGranted, and returns NotProcessedYet when the player is gone, which is the shape Roblox asks for and avoids charge-without-item complaints.
- Revive is refused before the prompt when the player is alive or between waves (canRevive), so the only revive complaint left is timing, not double charging.
- Hit feedback baseline is strong: floating damage numbers (red and bigger on kills) in HitMarkers.client.luau, hit and kill sounds, camera shake and FOV kick, muzzle flash, tracers, hit sparks and death bursts in each enemy's accent color with parts flying apart for 1.2 s (EnemyAI.luau, Effects.client.luau, ClientFX.luau). No blood, as required.
- Boss moment is staged properly: BossIntro.client.luau does letterbox bars, a 4 s scriptable camera sweep on the boss gate and a red name card; the roar and thunder play; the boss HP bar sits on the HUD with the name; boss death gets a 90-particle burst, a 30-stud ring, shake 5 and a FOV kick.
- Enemy unlock cadence is right for a first session: Walker only on wave 1, Runners at 2, Flyers at 3, boss at 5, Tanks at 6, Spitters at 8; each boss has a different mechanic (Brute contact, Queen summons Runners, Sky King flies and shoots). Enemies.weightsForWave is tested.
- Player damage state reads well: three-color HP bar, pulsing red low-HP vignette under 35%, hurt flash and shake on every hit, revive button only appears when dead mid-wave.
