# Swarm Break: story and world (plain-words version, 2026-09-30)

**Short version:** Outpost 9 is a mining station on an ice moon. The drills broke into a hollow under the ice: the **Nest**. The Swarm came out. You are a **Survivor**. You hold the platform, wave after wave, with weapons that run on the Nest's own energy.

## The world
- **Outpost 9**: a steel platform of floor tiles, walls, pillars, crates and lamps built over the ice. Under the grates you can see purple **Swarm energy** glowing up from the Nest.
- **The Nest**: the hive under the station. Spawn holes in the floor are cracked rims with purple light and roots creeping over the edge.
- **Swarm energy**: the purple glow. It powers the enemies and, once you learn to use it, your weapons. Weapon evolution is you bending that energy into one of four elements.

## The Swarm (enemies)
- **Walker**: hunched, armoured, gorilla stance, orange eyes. Slow, hits hard.
- **Runner**: low four-legged sprinter with a spined back and teal lights. Fast, weak.
- **Flyer**: wasp-lantern with big clear wings and a glowing yellow tail. Dives.
- **Tank**: beetle with a spiked steel dome and green seams. Soaks damage.
- **Spitter**: hunched bug with purple acid sacs on its back. Spits from range.
- **Big Brute** (wave 5): a Walker grown huge, spikes down its back, a red crown of light.
- **Swarm Queen** (wave 10): a walking nest; her abdomen is studded with glowing eggs. Hatches Runners while she lives.
- **Sky King** (wave 15): a giant floating eye in armour, a halo of blue light, four wings. Rains fire from above.

## The four elements (weapon evolution)
One path per weapon, three tiers each. Costs Gems plus shards of that element.

| Element | Feel | Tier 2 effect | Tier 3 effect | Shards from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | green vines and spores | Heal on kill | Poison: slows enemies | Swarm Queen |
| **Tech** | cyan circuit lines | Hits bounce to a second enemy | Hits bounce (stronger) | every 5th wave cleared |
| **Lightning** | white-blue crackle | Chain lightning | Faster movement | Sky King |
| **Dark** | purple smoke | Lifesteal (heal on hit) | Kills pull enemies together | Big Brute |

## Currencies
- **Coins**: earned in a run, spent in a run on upgrades. Reset every run. (Robux coin packs top these up.)
- **Gems**: earned at the end of a run from the wave you reached. Kept forever. Spent in the Base on evolutions and perks.
- **Shards**: one kind per element (Nature, Tech, Lightning, Dark). Drop from bosses and wave clears as above.

## The Base (lobby)
Where you land first, with up to 30 players. Zones, each with its own look:
- **Weapons**: equip and evolve (the four element pillars glow in their colors).
- **Perks**: spend Gems on permanent boosts (a workshop bench).
- **Crates**: open weapon crates; odds shown on the wall.
- **Passes**: the Robux kiosk.
- **Play pads**: stand on a pad with friends, the countdown runs, you drop into an 8-player arena.
- **Leaderboard**: the wall of best waves.

## Tone
No blood. Bugs burst into sparks and light in their own color. Bosses get an intro card and a roar. Dark station, bright lights, big effects.
